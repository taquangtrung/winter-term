# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about each other.

## What is expected

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, is expected to:

- Give and take criticism about the work without making it about the person doing it.
- Accept that other people have different experience, hardware, shells, workflows, and levels of familiarity with the codebase.
- Say when you are unsure. "I do not know" is a useful contribution.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to decline a change. Winter is early software maintained by one person in their spare time, and scope has to be said no to.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, whether in public or in private.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: repeatedly reopening settled decisions, or badgering people for free work.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any channel that represents the project. It also applies when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to <taquangtrungvn@gmail.com>. Reports are read by the maintainer and are not shared further without the reporter's agreement, except where the maintainer is legally required to.

Reporting in good faith will never count against you, even if the report turns out to be a misunderstanding.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will respond in a way that fits what happened: a private word, a public correction, editing or removing a comment, or blocking someone from the project. A first-time misstep that someone owns and corrects is not the same as a pattern, and will not be treated as one.

If you disagree with a decision, say so by email. There is no separate appeals body; this is a small project.
